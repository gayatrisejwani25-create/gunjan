📐 Understanding the Chain Rule

The aim of this virtual lab is to understand and apply the Chain Rule for differentiating composite functions. Students will explore how the derivative of a composite function f(g(x)) is the product of the derivative of the outer function evaluated at the inner function times the derivative of the inner function.
